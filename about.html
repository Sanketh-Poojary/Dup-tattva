Understanding taxonomy

How life is classified, why scientific names matter, and how to read the results from this tool.

What is taxonomy?

Taxonomy is the science of naming, describing, and classifying living things. Every known organism is placed into a hierarchy of categories, from the broadest group — Kingdom — down to the most specific, Species. This hierarchy lets us see how plants and animals are related and communicate about them unambiguously across languages.

The classification hierarchy

Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species

Common vs. scientific names

Common Name

Varies by region and language — "mango," "aam," "manga." Easy to say, but one plant can have dozens of common names and one name can refer to several plants.

Scientific Name

A unique two-part Latin name — Mangifera indica — used worldwide. The first part is the genus, the second the species. Always written in italics.
