Project Overview

Behind the Explorer

Tattva is an advanced biological taxonomy tool designed to bring the hidden hierarchy of the natural world to your fingertips.

What is Taxonomy?

Taxonomy is the science of naming, describing, and classifying living organisms. Every known plant, animal, fungus, and microbe is placed into a hierarchy of categories, from the broadest group — Kingdom — down to the most specific, Species.

This hierarchy, originally developed by Carl Linnaeus, allows scientists and nature enthusiasts to see how different forms of life are related evolutionarily, and provides a universal language (Scientific Names) that bypasses regional language barriers.

Data Sources

Tattva aggregates real-time data to provide accurate, up-to-date scientific classifications.

  • iNaturalist API Provides the core taxonomic tree, organism images, and global observation counts.
  • Wikipedia Open Data Fetches detailed, peer-reviewed summaries for deep-dive biological context.
